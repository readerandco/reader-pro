Service Terms

Last updated: 17 November 2026

These terms cover the counting service provided by Reader & Co., run by its two co-founders in Patna, India. They are separate from the terms of the Reader Pro app.

What we do

You send us a sheet of links; we send it back counted. Two services:

  • Reel & video counting - for Instagram, Facebook and YouTube links, we fill in the exact Views, Likes, Comments and Shares. Where your sheet asks for it, we also add the author's followers or subscribers.
  • Link Collection - you give us profile links and a date range; we find every reel or video posted in that range and write the links, with their posted dates, into your sheet.

How the counts are read

Every count is read inside the platform's own app at the time of counting - the same numbers anyone opening the link would see. A number the platform does not show is left blank. We never guess a count, and we never fill an unread number with zero.

Prices

  • Counting: Rs 80 per 200 links (Rs 0.40 per link), charged per link read.
  • Link Collection: Rs 1 for every link found. A profile with nothing posted in your date range costs nothing.
  • The same link appearing twice in a sheet is counted once.
  • Dead links - removed posts, broken links, links that no longer open - are never charged.

Your first test sheet is free

Before your first paid job, we count one test sheet free. Send a sheet big enough to judge our work properly - that is what it is for. Standard prices apply from the next sheet.

Time

Before we start a job, we agree the delivery time with you, based on the size of the sheet and your deadline. We do not promise a fixed time before seeing the sheet.

What you receive

Every job is delivered with four things: the counted sheet, a delivery note, a receipt, and a list of any dead links found. Receipts carry an RC- serial number; they are receipts, not GST invoices.

Payment

Payment is by UPI or a Razorpay payment link. Our UPI currently shows the name MD AHMAD - that is us. A job is complete on delivery; if we ever cannot complete a paid job, the amount paid for the unfinished part is returned.

Counts are live numbers

Counts are true at the moment they are read. Posts keep gaining views after delivery, so a later look will usually show higher numbers. That does not make the delivered count wrong.

Your sheets

Your sheets are used only for your job. They are never shared, and they are never uploaded to any third-party counting service.

Changes

We may update these terms from time to time. The version on this page when you place an order is the version that applies to that order.
